Teacher's Day Interactive Invitation
======================================

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js
- assets/invitation-card.png
- assets/skf-logo.jpg

Flow:
1. Closed envelope appears.
2. Click the seal.
3. Envelope opens and "You are Invited!" letter rises.
4. The exact supplied invitation-card.png is revealed.
5. Confetti animation runs.
6. The exact supplied invitation card automatically downloads.
7. A manual download button remains available.

Run:
Open index.html in a browser. For best download behavior when deploying,
use a normal web server/hosting rather than file://.

To change the invitation:
Replace assets/invitation-card.png with your final card image.
